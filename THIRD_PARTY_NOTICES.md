# Third-Party Notices

Magelaxiom is designed for open-source publication and avoids proprietary
dictionary services.

## Open English WordNet

Website: https://en-word.net/

Repository: https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet and includes Princeton
WordNet attribution and license requirements.

Use in this app: build-time conversion of WNDB files into an embedded TSV and
binary index for definitions, synonym sets, and antonym relations. No runtime
API calls are made.

Required attribution summary:

- Open English WordNet Team, Open English WordNet, https://en-word.net/
- Princeton University, Princeton WordNet
- Open English WordNet is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- Princeton WordNet-derived material remains subject to the Princeton WordNet
  license terms included in the Open English WordNet distribution.

## Manual Phrase Fallbacks

Magelaxiom also includes a small manually authored phrase fallback table for
common Latin and economic/legal phrases that are useful in dictionary lookup.
